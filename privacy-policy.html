Privacy Policy for Currency Pulse

Package ID: com.rmok.currencypulse (Android & Wear OS) · Effective Date: November 2026
Summary: Currency Pulse does not require user accounts, does not show third-party ads, and processes all camera price tag scanning 100% on your device.

1. Camera & AI Price Tag Scanning (On-Device Only)

Currency Pulse requests the CAMERA permission solely when you use the Price Tag Scanner feature.

  • Camera frames are analyzed locally on your device using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition to extract numeric prices and currency symbols.
  • No photos, video streams, or recognized text are ever uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

2. Photo Gallery / Screenshots

When you explicitly tap the Screenshot button inside the Price Tag Scanner, Currency Pulse creates a single composite PNG image containing the current tag view and conversion readout and saves it locally to your device Gallery under Pictures/CurrencyPulse using Android MediaStore. Currency Pulse does not read or access any other photos in your Gallery.

3. Exchange Rate & Historical Data Requests

When online and refreshing exchange rates or viewing 1-year historical charts, the app makes standard HTTPS requests to public exchange-rate APIs (open.er-api.com and api.frankfurter.dev).

  • Currency Pulse does not attach your name, email address, location, or advertising ID to these requests.
  • Cached rates are stored locally on your device so the app continues working offline.

4. Local App Preferences

Your selected 6 currencies, display order, active input amount, history pair, and free-scan trial counter are saved locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and are never transmitted externally by us.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Currency Pulse offers an optional one-time upgrade (Currency Pulse Pro, SKU pro_unlock) after 10 complimentary AI Price Tag Scans.

  • All payment processing and purchase verification are handled directly by Google Play Billing.
  • We never receive or store your credit card number, billing address, or financial account details. Google Play's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Wear OS Companion App

The standalone Wear OS version of Currency Pulse for Samsung Galaxy Watch and compatible Wear OS devices follows the same policy: no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, and no personal data collection.

7. Children's Privacy

Currency Pulse does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If future versions of Currency Pulse introduce new features that affect data handling, this policy will be updated here before those updates are released on Google Play.
